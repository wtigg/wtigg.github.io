The service

Rankly shows changes to public app store listings of the apps you choose, review themes, a localization map and a weekly memo. Data comes from public listings and may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Rank movements shown next to a change coincide with it in time; Rankly never claims a change caused them.

AI content

Summaries, screenshot readings, themes, memos and reply drafts are generated by AI and may contain mistakes. Check them before acting on them. Reply drafts use placeholders in [brackets] for facts only you know; Rankly never posts anything for you.

Subscriptions

Rankly Pro is offered as a weekly or yearly auto-renewable subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and you can manage or cancel them in your App Store account settings. A free trial, when offered, converts to a paid subscription at its end unless canceled; any unused part of a trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription.

Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement applies.

Acceptable use

Use Rankly for your own market research. Do not resell its data or use it to harass developers or reviewers.
