How often are rankings updated?

Rankly checks the Top 100 of every tracked chart in all 175 App Store countries about every hour. Apple's own charts can lag behind.

My app is not ranked

Rankly shows countries where your app is in the Top 100 of its category, or of all apps. If it is not in any Top 100 yet, it appears as soon as it charts.

I don't get alerts

Alerts are part of Rankly Pro. Check that notifications are allowed for Rankly in iOS Settings, and that alerts are on for the app in Rankly's Settings tab.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in Rankly and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Rankly.
