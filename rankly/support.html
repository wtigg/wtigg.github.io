Support

Contact

Questions, bugs, or a removal request (a review, a listing, or all data linked to your device): write to soubigoupaul@gmail.com. Include a link to the item when you ask for a removal.

Why don't I see changes yet?

Rankly reads each listing once a day. A change shows up after it has been seen twice in a row, so store tests and glitches don't flood your feed.

Why is a rival marked "Paused"?

The free plan watches your app and your first 2 rivals in 1 storefront. Pro watches up to 10 rivals in 15 storefronts.

Restore a purchase

Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Rankly.
