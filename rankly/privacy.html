Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The short version

Rankly has no account and no sign-in, and it contains no analytics or advertising trackers. It only stores what it needs to scan rankings for the apps you follow and to send you alerts.

What our server stores

  • A random identifier created by the app on your device. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID.
  • The App Store IDs of the apps you track, and whether alerts are on for each.
  • If you allow notifications: your Apple push token and its environment, used only to send ranking alerts.
  • Your device language and whether you have Rankly Pro, to write alerts in your language and send them to subscribers.

Rankings, ratings and reviews come from public App Store data published by Apple. They are stored on our server (Supabase, hosted in the European Union) to build ranking history.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check your subscription status. RevenueCat receives the random app identifier and your purchase receipts from Apple, never your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

iCloud

If iCloud is on, your list of tracked apps and pinned countries is synced between your devices through your own iCloud account. We cannot read it.

What we never do

  • No selling or sharing of data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos library content or camera. Saving a share card to Photos only adds the image you chose.

Retention and deletion

Ranking history is kept up to 120 days. When you remove an app, it stops being tracked for your device. To delete everything linked to your device identifier, email us from the app's Support link and we will erase it within 30 days.

Children

Rankly is a tool for app developers and is not directed at children under 13.
