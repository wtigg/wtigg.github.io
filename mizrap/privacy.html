Privacy policy

Mızrap · last updated November 4, 2026

Mızrap is a tuner for makam and maqam instruments. You don't create an account. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.

Microphone

Mızrap uses the microphone only to hear your strings and measure their pitch and beats. The sound is analysed in real time on your iPhone. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The app asks for microphone access only after you tap Continue, and you can turn it off in iOS Settings, Mızrap.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your instrument, tuning, ahenk or karar, A4 calibration and display unit.
  • Your onboarding answers and settings.

Deleting the app removes this data.

Subscription

Purchases go through Apple's App Store. To check your subscription or lifetime purchase, Mızrap uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app and the purchase details Apple provides (product, dates, status). We never receive your name, email address or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Remote setting

At launch the app reads one technical setting from our server (Supabase) to know whether it may ask you to rate the app. No personal data is sent. As with any connection, the server sees your IP address for the time of the request.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the app schedules one local reminder the day before the trial ends. It is created on your iPhone and nothing is sent to us.

Children

Mızrap is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

We hold no data about you apart from anonymous purchase records, so deleting the app removes almost everything. For any question about privacy or the GDPR, write to soubigoupaul@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
