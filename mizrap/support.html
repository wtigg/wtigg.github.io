Support

Write to soubigoupaul@gmail.com. Tell us your instrument and tuning. Corrections from players and teachers are welcome: every tuning, makam and usul is open to review.

The needle doesn't move

Check that Mızrap has microphone access in iOS Settings, Mızrap. Pluck one string at a time, close to the iPhone, in a quiet room.

The note name looks wrong

Some instruments are written at a different pitch than they sound (tambur, Turkish oud, Turkish kanun). Mızrap shows the written name, with the sounding pitch as a caption. Check the ahenk or karar in the tuning settings.

Koma or cents?

Turkish instruments can show deviations in koma (53 per octave) or in cents. Pick the unit in Settings (Premium).

Restore my purchase

Cancel my subscription

Settings, then Manage subscription, or iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The lifetime purchase never renews.
