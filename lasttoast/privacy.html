Privacy policy

Last Toast · last updated November 4, 2026

Last Toast is a murder-mystery party kit for iPhone. You don't create an account. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no server that receives your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The first names you type for your guests and the she or he choice for each.
  • The party date, the roles dealt, the progress of the evening, the votes and the scores.
  • Your onboarding answers and settings.

This stays in the app's storage on your iPhone. It is deleted when you clear the party or delete the app. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, it is included in that backup like any other app data, under Apple's terms.

What you share

Invitations and role booklets are created on your iPhone. They leave the device only when you send them yourself through the share sheet (Messages, Mail, AirDrop and so on). From there, the app you choose and the recipient handle them.

Purchases

Cases are one-time purchases made through Apple's App Store. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple tells the app which cases your Apple ID owns so it can unlock them.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules local notifications on your iPhone when an act timer ends. They are created on the device and nothing is sent to us.

Children

Last Toast is a party game for teenagers and adults. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, deleting the app removes everything. For any question about privacy or the GDPR, write to soubigoupaul@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
