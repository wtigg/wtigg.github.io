Before the party

How many players?
The Last Toast plays with 4 to 6, Speakeasy 1927 with 6 to 12. You play too.

Who is the culprit?
The app deals the roles at random and never shows you who did it. You find out with everyone else at the reveal.

Invitations
Each guest gets a text with their character and a costume idea, sent from your phone.

Role booklets
You can send each guest a PDF booklet instead of passing your phone around. Send each one straight to its guest and don't open it: it holds their secrets.

On the night

Passing the phone
At each reading round, a guest taps their name and holds the pad to read. Letting go hides the text.

Timers
Each act has one clock. It rings, buzzes and, if you allowed notifications, notifies you even when the phone is locked.

Screen mirroring
Private screens hide while the iPhone is mirrored or recorded.

Pausing
Tap pause at the top left. Your place is saved.

Purchases

Restore
Library, then Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.

Ask to Buy
The case unlocks once the purchase is approved.

Refunds
