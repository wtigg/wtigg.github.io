Privacy policy

Gurumi · last updated November 4, 2026

Gurumi turns a shape, a size and your yarn into an amigurumi pattern. You don't create an account. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your projects: shapes, sizes, yarn, hook, gauge disc measurements, and the round and stitch you reached.
  • Your onboarding answers and settings (units, pattern terminology).
  • The follow-along counter shown in the Live Activity and widget, shared between the app and its widget on your iPhone only.

Patterns, charts and 3D previews are computed on your iPhone. Deleting a project or the app removes this data. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, it is included in that backup like any other app data, under Apple's terms.

What you share

PDF and text exports are created on your iPhone and leave it only when you send them yourself through the share sheet.

Subscription

Purchases go through Apple's App Store. To check your subscription, Gurumi uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app and the purchase details Apple provides (product, dates, status). We never receive your name, email address or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Gurumi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

We hold no data about you apart from anonymous purchase records, so deleting the app removes almost everything. For any question about privacy or the GDPR, write to soubigoupaul@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
