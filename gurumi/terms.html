The app

Gurumi computes crochet patterns from the shape, size and gauge you give it. Stitch counts are calculated, and real results depend on your yarn, hook and tension: check your gauge with the calibration disc. The patterns you generate are yours to crochet, give away or sell as finished pieces.

Free and Pro

The free version includes the ball and the cylinder up to 8 cm, the terms of your device language and the follow-along counter. Gurumi Pro unlocks every shape and size, creatures, the circular chart, PDF export, resizing, the Live Activity and every terminology.
