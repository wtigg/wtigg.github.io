Support

Write to soubigoupaul@gmail.com. Tell us the shape, the size and your yarn if a pattern looks wrong.

My piece comes out bigger or smaller

Your tension sets the size. Crochet the 6-round calibration disc, measure it and enter the diameter: Gurumi recomputes every round from your own gauge.

US or UK terms?

Settings lets you pick US, UK, French, German, Spanish, Italian or Japanese pattern terms. The free version uses the terms of your device language.

The counter on my Lock Screen

Start following a pattern and allow Live Activities for Gurumi in iOS Settings. You can add a stitch from the Lock Screen.

Restore my subscription

Settings, then Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel my subscription

Settings, then Manage subscription, or iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
