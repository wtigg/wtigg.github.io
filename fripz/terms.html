What the app is

Fripz helps you prepare listings for second-hand clothing marketplaces: it suggests a title, description, brand, size, condition, category and price from your photos, cleans up your photos on your device, and helps you paste the listing into the Vinted app with the Fripz keyboard.

Fripz does not log in to Vinted, does not publish anything for you and does not automate the Vinted website. You publish every listing yourself.
