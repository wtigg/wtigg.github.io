Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The short version

Fripz has no account and no sign-in. Your listings and photos are stored on your iPhone. The only thing that leaves your device is the set of photos you choose to analyze, sent once to write your listing, and not kept.

Photos sent for analysis

When you create a listing, Fripz sends up to five of the photos you picked, plus your language, region, currency and any notes you typed, to our server (Supabase). The server forwards them to Google Gemini, an AI service by Google, which returns a suggested title, description, brand, size, condition, category and price. Photos are processed in real time and are not stored on our server once the listing is written.

Do not include people's faces or personal documents in your photos if you do not want them processed.

Photo studio

Background removal and photo enhancement run entirely on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device frameworks. These photos are not uploaded. Fripz never generates or alters the item itself with AI.

What stays on your device

  • Your listings, photos, prices and drafts.
  • Your preferences, such as language, tone and currency.

If you delete the app, all of it is removed.

Camera and photo library

Fripz asks for camera access to photograph items, photo library access to import photos, and permission to add photos so your listing photos are ready to pick inside Vinted. You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Fripz keyboard

The Fripz keyboard inserts the fields of your current listing into the Vinted app. It needs Full Access only to read that listing from the Fripz app on the same iPhone. The keyboard has no letter keys, does not log what you type, does not read other text fields and sends nothing over the network.

Device identifier

Requests to our server include the identifier for vendor, a random identifier assigned by Apple to this app on your device. It is used only to operate the service and prevent abuse, never for advertising or tracking.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription provider. RevenueCat processes a random app-generated identifier and your purchase receipt to unlock and restore your subscription. It does not receive your photos or listings. We never see your payment details.

Analytics and advertising

Fripz includes no analytics or advertising SDK. We do not track you across apps or websites and we do not sell or share your data.

Third-party services

  • Google Gemini (AI analysis): Google Privacy Policy
  • Supabase (server infrastructure): Supabase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (subscriptions): RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of personal data. Because Fripz keeps no account and does not store your photos, we generally hold no personal data about you. You can delete any listing in the app, and deleting the app removes everything.

Children

Fripz is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
