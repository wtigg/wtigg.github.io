How do I turn on the Fripz keyboard?

Open iOS Settings, then General, Keyboard, Keyboards, Add New Keyboard, and pick Fripz. Tap Fripz in the list and turn on Allow Full Access. Full Access only lets the keyboard read your current listing from the Fripz app.

How do I paste my listing into Vinted?

In Fripz, tap Publish on Vinted. Your photos are saved at the top of your camera roll and Vinted opens. Add the photos, then tap a text field, switch to the Fripz keyboard with the globe key, and tap the matching button: title, description, brand or price. Pick category, size, condition, colour and material in Vinted's own lists.

The keyboard says there is no listing

Make sure Allow Full Access is on, then open the listing in Fripz and tap Publish on Vinted again.

The photo studio did not remove the background

Background removal runs on your iPhone and works best with the item flat or on a hanger, well lit, against a plain background. If it cannot find the item, Fripz keeps an enhanced, cropped version of your original photo.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, select Fripz, then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

I paid but the app still asks me to subscribe

Open the paywall and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID as for the purchase. If it still does not unlock, email us with the date of purchase.

Will I lose my listings if I change iPhone?

Yes. Fripz has no account and stores listings on the device, so they do not transfer to a new phone and are lost if you delete the app.

Is Fripz made by Vinted?

No. Fripz is an independent app, not affiliated with Vinted.
