Privacy policy

Under the Lamp · effective November 4, 2026

Under the Lamp is an interrogation game. You don't create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.

Google Gemini

Suspects answer through Google Gemini, an AI service operated by Google. For each question we send Google the question, the last few exchanges with that suspect, and the parts of the written case file the suspect may know. We do not send your purchase ID, device ID or IP address to Google. The app asks for your consent before the first interrogation, and you can withdraw it in Settings. Google processes this data under the Gemini API terms.

Voice

If you ask questions out loud, speech is turned into text on your iPhone by Apple's on-device recognition. The audio never leaves the device. Only the text you choose to send is treated as above.

On your iPhone

Your grades, onboarding answers and settings stay on your iPhone. Settings › Reset case progress clears them; deleting the app removes them.

Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete the data linked to your purchase ID. Because we don't know who you are, include the Support ID shown in the app's Settings. Everything is deleted automatically after 30 days anyway. Under the GDPR you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Under the Lamp is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13.
