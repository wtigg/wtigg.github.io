How do I break a lie?

Talk to find what doesn't add up, then open the evidence tray and put the card that contradicts the story on the table in front of that suspect. Showing a card costs one question. Asking alone never unlocks a secret.

I ran out of questions

Each case has 30. When they're gone, make your accusation: who, why, and which card proves it.

Restore my subscription

Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel my subscription

Settings › Manage subscription, or your Apple ID settings in the iOS Settings app.

Start a case over

After a verdict, tap "Play this case again" (Premium). Settings › Reset case progress clears all grades on this iPhone.

A suspect said something inappropriate

Press and hold the reply, then "Report this reply". It is hidden on your screen and sent to us for review.

Asking out loud doesn't work

Voice needs on-device speech recognition for English, and microphone and speech recognition access in iOS Settings › Under the Lamp.
