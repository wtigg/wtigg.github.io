Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

The short version

Same Place never uploads your photos. It reads only two pieces of metadata from them — when the photo was taken and roughly where — and compares those points with the person you paired with. There is no account, no email, and no sign-in.

What the app reads

With your permission, Same Place reads the creation date and the GPS coordinates stored in your photo library's metadata. It does not read, copy, upload, or analyze the image content of your photos. Photo files stay on your device at all times.

What is shared with your pair

When you link with someone, each device uploads a list of points to our database, and each point holds only a latitude, a longitude, a timestamp, and a random device identifier. That list is scoped to the pair you created. Nothing in it identifies you by name, email, or Apple Account, and no photo, thumbnail, or filename is ever sent.

Your identity

Same Place does not use accounts. On first launch the app generates a random identifier that lives only on your device and in the pairs you join. A first name and a home city are optional, stored locally, and used purely to label things inside the app.

Where the data lives

Pair records and the anonymized points are stored on Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the European Union. Matches, connections, and settings are also cached on your device. Deleting the app removes the local copy.

Deleting your data

Email paul@rocapine.com with the pair code shown in the app and we will delete that pair and every point attached to it. Because the app holds no account, that code is the only way to identify your data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat processes a random app-generated identifier and your purchase receipt in order to unlock premium features. It receives no photo data and no location data.

Location permission

If you choose to set your home city from your current position, the app uses your location once for that purpose. Same Place does not track your location in the background.

No trackers

Same Place contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics that profile you.

Children

Same Place is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email paul@rocapine.com.
