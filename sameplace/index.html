Same Place

Every moment your paths crossed — long before you met.

What it does

Same Place compares the metadata of your photos with your partner's, your friend's, or a family member's: the date and the GPS coordinates, never the pictures themselves. From those two timelines it finds the places where you were in the same spot at the same time, without ever knowing it.

How it works

  • Invite your person with a link. Both devices stay anonymous.
  • Each phone reads its own photo metadata locally and shares only coarse points (latitude, longitude, timestamp).
  • Same Place matches the two sets and reveals the crossings, with a map and a story for each one.

Privacy first

Your photos never leave your phone. There is no account, no email, no sign-in. Read the full privacy policy.
