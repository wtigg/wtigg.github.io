Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

The short version

Urge keeps your spending data on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in, and no email required. Nothing you log leaves your device.

What stays on your device

  • The items you log, their prices, categories and cooldown timers.
  • Your decisions to buy or skip, and your savings total.
  • Your daily check-ins, moods, reflections and streaks.
  • Your name, savings goal, currency and profile photo.

All of it is stored inside the app. If you delete the app, that data is removed with it.

Not financial advice

Urge helps you pause and reflect on spending decisions. It does not provide financial, investment, credit, tax or legal advice, and it is not a substitute for a qualified professional.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat processes a random app-generated identifier and your purchase receipt in order to unlock and restore your subscription. It does not receive your logged items or your check-ins.

Analytics

Urge does not include any analytics or advertising SDK. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not use an advertising identifier.

Children

Urge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can delete any logged item at any time. Resetting the app from Settings erases all local data and cancels pending notifications. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
