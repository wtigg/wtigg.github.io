Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

The short version

Urge keeps your spending data on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in, and no email required. The only thing that leaves your device is what you type to the coach, and only while you are using it.

What stays on your device

  • The items you log, their prices, categories and cooldown timers.
  • Your decisions to buy or skip, and your savings total.
  • Your daily check-ins, moods, reflections and streaks.
  • Your name, savings goal, currency and profile photo.

All of it is stored inside the app. If you delete the app, that data is removed with it.

The AI coach

When you send a message to the coach, that message is sent to our server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to produce a reply. To make the reply useful, the request also includes a short summary of your current context: your first name if you set one, your savings total and streak, your savings goal, and the items currently in cooldown with their prices.

Each request carries a random identifier generated on your device. It is not linked to your Apple ID, your email, or any account. We do not store your conversations on our servers. Your conversation history is kept only on your device, and you can clear it at any time from the coach screen.

Google processes these requests as our service provider. See Google's API terms and privacy policy for how they handle data sent to the Gemini API.

The coach is not a professional

The coach offers perspective and reflection on spending decisions. It does not provide financial, investment, credit, tax or legal advice, and it is not a substitute for a qualified professional.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat processes a random app-generated identifier and your purchase receipt in order to unlock and restore your subscription. It does not receive your logged items, your check-ins or your conversations.

Analytics

The app may collect anonymous usage events, such as which screens are opened and whether onboarding was completed, to understand how the app is used and to measure advertising campaigns. These events do not include your logged items, your check-ins or your conversations. If you decline app tracking when iOS asks, no advertising identifier is used.

Children

Urge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can delete any logged item at any time. Deleting your account from Settings erases all local data and cancels pending notifications. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
