How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, select Urge, then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

I paid but the app still asks me to subscribe

Open the paywall and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase. If it still does not unlock, email us with the date of purchase.

Will I lose my data if I change iPhone?

Yes. Urge stores everything on the device and has no account system, so your logged items, streak and check-ins do not transfer to a new phone and are lost if you delete the app.

The coach is not replying

The coach needs an internet connection. If the error persists on a good connection, email us.

Is the coach a real advisor?

No. It is an AI that helps you think a purchase through. It does not give financial, investment, credit or tax advice. For money trouble, speak to a qualified professional or a debt counselling service in your country.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in the app and tap Delete Account. This erases everything stored on the device. Deleting the app also removes it.
